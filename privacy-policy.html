Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Meetrohub ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Meetrohub mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect user information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

When you register or use Meetrohub, we may collect the following information provided directly by you:

  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely in encrypted form)
  • Organization name and organization code
  • Meeting room booking details (date, time, room, duration)
  • Profile information such as your name
  • Profile picture uploaded by the user (optional)

Uploading a profile picture is optional and entirely controlled by the user.

b. Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary for app functionality and stability, such as:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Basic app usage data (e.g., app crashes, performance logs)

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, SMS, call logs, or any sensitive personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for legitimate app functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Authenticating users securely
  • Displaying user profile information within the organization
  • Facilitating organization-based meeting room bookings
  • Preventing double bookings and scheduling conflicts
  • Maintaining app security, stability, and performance

3. Profile Pictures

If a user chooses to upload a profile picture:

  • The image is used only for identification within the app
  • The image is visible only to users within the same organization
  • The image is not used for advertising or marketing purposes

Users may update or remove their profile picture at any time from within the app.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

User information, including profile pictures, is not shared with third parties except:

  • When required by law
  • To protect the security, integrity, or rights of the app and its users

5. Ads and Tracking

Meetrohub does not display advertisements.

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.

We do not track users across apps or websites.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data, including secure authentication and controlled access to stored information.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

Meetrohub is intended for individuals aged 18 years and above.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
