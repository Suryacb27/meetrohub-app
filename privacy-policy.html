Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Meetrohub ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Meetrohub mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

When you use Meetrohub, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely in encrypted form)
  • Organization name and organization code
  • Meeting room booking details (date, time, room)

b. Automatically Collected Information

We may collect basic technical information such as:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage data necessary for functionality and stability

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, SMS, call logs, or any sensitive personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information only to:

  • Provide and operate the Meetrohub app
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts
  • Enable organization-based meeting room bookings
  • Prevent double bookings and scheduling conflicts
  • Maintain app security and performance

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

Your information is not shared with third parties, except:

  • When required to comply with legal obligations
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the app

4. Ads and Tracking

Meetrohub does not display advertisements.

We do not use AdMob or third-party advertising SDKs.

We do not track users across apps or websites.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including secure authentication and access controls.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials. Please notify us immediately if you believe your account has been compromised.

7. Children’s Privacy

Meetrohub is intended for use by individuals aged 18 years and above.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
