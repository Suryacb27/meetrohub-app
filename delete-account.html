Request Account Deletion

Users of the Meetrohub mobile application can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting us using the method described below.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Meetrohub account and associated data, please send an email from your registered email address to:

Email: support@meetrohub.com

Please include the following details in your email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your organization name (if applicable)
  • A clear request to delete your account

Once we receive your request, we will verify your identity and process the deletion within a reasonable timeframe, as required by applicable laws.

Some data may be retained temporarily where required for legal, security, or administrative purposes.
